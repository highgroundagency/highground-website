Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

High Ground Agency ("High Ground," "we," "us") is a marketing and content agency based in Aptos, California. This policy explains what information we collect through highgroundagency.net and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you email us, book or attend a call, or become a client, we receive what you share — typically your name, email address, phone number, business name, and details about your business and marketing.
  • Information from our ads. We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. If you fill out a lead form on one of our ads, Meta sends us the details you submit (such as your name, email, and phone number).
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs standard data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies. If we add tools such as website analytics or the Meta Pixel in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google and Google receives your IP address as part of that request.

How we use information

  • To reply to you and schedule calls
  • To provide, manage, and improve our marketing services
  • To send follow-up messages about our services, which you can opt out of at any time
  • To keep the website secure and working
  • To meet legal and tax obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the business — for example email and file storage (Google Workspace), website hosting (Vercel), and advertising platforms (Meta) — who may only use it to provide their services to us.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients, or others.
  • A successor business if High Ground is ever sold or merged, subject to this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and client information for as long as we have a relationship with you and for a reasonable period afterward for records, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop sending you marketing messages. Email contact@highgroundagency.net and we will respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under California law, including the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information. We honor those requests from anyone, regardless of where you live.

Text messages and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

Children

Our website and services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the effective date above.
