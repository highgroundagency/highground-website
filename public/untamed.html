Stop 3 · The words in your ads

A few words could get your ads shut off

Meta treats skin-condition and medical words as health claims. Those ads get rejected or quietly shown to fewer people. Here are your real lines, before and after.

So why will people still buy?

They buy the feeling, not the diagnosis

“Soft”, “calm”, “finally works” is what people want. Nobody needs the medical word to picture it.

Proof does the persuading

Reviews, a real founder, texture shots and the ranch make the case harder than any claim can, and Meta can't reject them.

Ads that stay on win

A slightly softer line that keeps running all month beats a bold one that gets pulled in a week.

One of his own reviews says the balm healed a foot ulcer. Great for him to hear, but it must never go in an ad or on the product page.

Four ways to spend the same budget

Meta needs about 50 sales a week in an ad set before it really knows who to show it to. How you split the money decides whether you ever get there.

Recommended

How well it should do

Rule of thumb: an ad set's daily budget ≈ cost per sale × 7.14 to reach 50 sales in 7 days. Test fairly with separate ad-set budgets; scale proven winners with one campaign budget. Don't switch off single ads inside an ad set; switch off whole ad sets after 3–7 days.

Phone settings

Just an iPhone

Vertical. 1080p, 30fps. Wipe the lens. Tap and hold to lock focus. Start recording 3 seconds early and stop 3 seconds late.

Sound

Keep it real

Real sound (wind, scraping, the lid twisting) beats music. Voice-overs go in a quiet room with the phone 6 inches away, like a voice memo.

Never want to be on camera? You don't have to be.

Founder ads are some of the strongest ads out there. Pick a step and stop there. You're already in the photos on your own homepage.

On AI: you're right to be careful

New York now fines ads with AI-made people that aren't labeled: $1,000, then $5,000 for each repeat. And AI video that's too clean looks fake to people scrolling. So here's the line we draw.

AI we won't use
  • ✕ AI people pretending to be customers
  • ✕ An AI “founder” or fake testimonials
  • ✕ Anything that could pass as a real person and isn't
AI that just saves time
  • ✓ Dozens of static ads made from your real product photos
  • ✓ Editing your real footage into finished videos
  • ✓ Writing several versions of each ad's text
  • ✓ Clearly cartoon or claymation ads nobody mistakes for real
