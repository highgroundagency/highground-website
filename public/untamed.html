So why will people still buy?

Sell the feeling

“Soft”, “calm”, “finally works” is what people want. Nobody needs the medical word to picture it.

A coach in our private Meta ads trainingPeople buy on emotion, justify with logic. So sell the feeling first and then hand them the logic afterwards.
Proof does the persuading

Reviews, a real founder, texture shots and the ranch make the case harder than any claim can, and Meta can't reject them.

Ads that stay on win

A slightly softer line that keeps running all month beats a bold one that gets pulled in a week.

How well we think it would do

Rule of thumb: an ad set's daily budget ≈ cost per sale × 7.14 to reach 50 sales in 7 days. Test fairly with separate ad-set budgets; scale proven winners with one campaign budget. Don't switch off single ads inside an ad set; switch off whole ad sets after 3–7 days.

On AI: you're right to be careful

If an ad shows AI-made people, it has to say so. New York now fines unlabeled AI people in ads: $1,000, then $5,000 for each repeat. And AI video that's too clean looks fake to people scrolling. So here's the line we draw.

The playbook we run onMake sure to disclose that the content features AI actors and not real people to comply with regulations and ethics.
The playbook we run onClaymation and Pixar-style ads. The most brand-safe AI ads, and they've been working well in health and supplements.
