Shoot day

The full shot list

All on a phone. Shoot video tall and keep you or the jar in the middle, so it crops to square too.

The jar

  1. Label straight to camera, plain background, no words. One jar, then two together.
  2. The same jar three ways: plain white, flat from above, on a counter or the rail.
  3. Open jar from above at an angle, lid beside it.So the balm shows.

Hands and texture

  1. Hands only, from your own eyes: open, scoop, rub into the back of the hand, in sun.
  2. Fingertip with a scoop of balm, close. A few shots straight down on one dark surface.
  3. Jar held up to the camera at chest height.

You

  1. Talking to camera at eye level while someone asks you questions. Indoors and at the ranch. Start and end with the jar in your hand.Five to ten different first lines.
  2. The pour in Soquel: pouring, labeling, packing. Close on hands, plus one wide of the room.
  3. Portrait, waist up, holding the jar. One tall, one wide.

Ranch and gifts

  1. Ranch wides and you with the animals, for your story.
  2. Two jars wrapped as a gift: box to camera, opening, one close-up, a real hand-off between two people.
  3. Creators film themselves outside: some selfie, some filmed by a friend, some full body.

Never: before and after photos, skin problems, or words printed on the photo.

Ranch photos tell your story. They don't go next to "how it's made", because the tallow comes from the farm in Illinois.
