A quick look before our meeting: what we found, what we'd do, and exactly what you'd get. I'll walk you through all of it, with examples, when we meet.

You built the real thing: a real ranch, real ingredients, real reviews. Most skincare brands have to fake that story. You lived it.

1What we found

  • Your ads say one thing. Four ads are live. Three lead with the same idea (your ingredients vs. the other stuff) and one is a candle. None show the ranch, your story, or a real customer.
  • Some ad wording is risky. Lines like “Nature's Botox”, “fight fungus” and “it's inflamed” read to Meta as health claims, which can get ads rejected. We keep the punch and change the words.
  • The product page loses people. A 10% pop-up covers the product on load. The 2-pack is $39.08 and free shipping starts at $40. Add to Cart is about two phone screens down, and reviews sit at the very bottom.
  • No social links. The site doesn't link to Instagram, TikTok or Facebook anywhere.

2What we'd do

  • Advertise your best seller, not all 18. Every ad points to your best-selling balm and its 2-pack, plus a gift set in December.
  • Two campaigns, same $500 a day. About $150 a day tests new ads every week; about $350 a day sits behind the ones already proven to sell.
  • UGC with you off camera. Real creators film on their phones from our scripts. Each one films three different openings, so one video becomes three ads to test.
  • Static ads from your own photos, in styles that look like posts, not ads.
  • Product page fixes first, then a new motion homepage in cycle 2.
  • Claymation or Pixar-style ads for the brand, if you want them.
